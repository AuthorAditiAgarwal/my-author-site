Do you want to **pivot quickly** to changing business needs? Do you want to **deliver business value faster** and **continually improve**? Do you want to **eliminate waste** and **optimize the end-to-end flow** of work? Do you want to **build high-quality products** in the shortest sustainable lead time? Do you want to **delight your customers** and **build motivated teams**? Do you want to create an environment of **mutual respect** and **trust**? Agile and Lean frameworks can **make it happen**!

 

By the end of this course, you will have an in-depth knowledge of the below topics:

- What Is Lean?
- House Of Lean
- Lean Principles
- What Is Agile?
- The Agile Manifesto
- Agile Principles based on the Agile Manifesto
- What Is Scaling Agile?
- Principles of Large-Scale Scrum (LeSS)
- Principles of Disciplined Agile (DA)
- Principles of Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe)

 

This course will help develop a Lean-Agile mindset for Release Train Engineers, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, Product Managers, Lean-Agile Leaders, Engineers, System and Solution Architects, and other SAFe or Agile practitioners. You will learn directly from an **Agile expert** and an author of several published books on Amazon!
