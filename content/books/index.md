- [**Enterprise Agility with OKRs**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VZ8QNXC): 
A practical guide for leaders looking to move beyond Agile practices to true enterprise agility. This book introduces a clear framework—the House of Enterprise Agility—covering planning, funding, teams, technology, leadership, and HR, all aligned through OKRs (Objectives and Key Results). With real-world examples and actionable guidance, it helps organizations connect strategy to execution and deliver measurable business outcomes.
